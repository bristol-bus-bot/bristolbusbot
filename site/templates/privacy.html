Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, adverts or analytics, and doesn't keep a log of who visits.

If you switch to night mode, your browser remembers that choice. It's stored on your device and never sent to me. If you use "find my location", your browser asks permission first, and your location is only used on your device to move the map. It isn't sent to the site.

The site runs behind Cloudflare, which, like any web host, sees your IP address in order to deliver the page. The map background tiles currently come from CARTO, which also sees your IP address when it sends them. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and CARTO's privacy policy.

Links to Bluesky, Instagram, GitHub and Ko-fi take you to those sites, and their own privacy policies apply once you're there.
